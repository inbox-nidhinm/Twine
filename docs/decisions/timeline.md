# The timeline

Decisions worth knowing about the timeline, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 28 October 2026 so they load only when needed. Keep each entry up to date when its code changes.

- **Timeline**: full width along the bottom in Animate (`EditorView`), height in `UserDefaults` (`EditorModel.timelineHeight`). `TimelineRow.rows` is one flat row per action in `ActionEditing.timeline` order: `rowOrder` is global across the project, a new action takes `ActionFactory.nextRowOrder(in:)` (the bottom), and dragging a label or bar renumbers with `ActionEditing.moving`. Continuous updates build on the current state, so a drag sends every row that differs from now, worked out from the rows as they are that moment (not the drawn ones). The header is the transport over `TimelineRow.labelWidth` (equal to `Theme.panelWidth`, so it lines up with the layers panel) and the ruler (`TimelineRulerView`: whole seconds with dots, the teal `Theme.playhead` pill with the time, the scene end to drag); the tracks (`TimelineTracksView`, an AppKit view in its own `NSScrollView`) draw labels (layer, action; the hovered label gets `Theme.rowHover` and its eye, and a switched-off action shows its crossed-out eye always, as a hidden layer's row does) in that column, with handles at the ends of the hovered bar marking the trim zones (`trimEdge`, reaching `edgeReach` in on long bars) and share `TimelineViewport` with the ruler, whose `start` never goes earlier than 0s less `TimelineViewport.lead`. Dragged and trimmed bars snap softly (`TimelineTracksView.snapped`, as the user asked): an edge within `snapDistance` (6 pt) of the playhead, the scene's start or end, or a bar's start or end on a row in view (`visibleRect`; rows scrolled away are ignored) lands on it, holds until pulled past `releaseDistance` (9 pt, soft on purpose), bumps the trackpad (`NSHapticFeedbackManager`, `.alignment`) as it catches, and draws a `Theme.guide` line from the bar it met to the dragged one (through the whole view for the playhead and scene ends). Command, or the canvas's Snapping switch (`EditorModel.snaps`), moves bars freely. Nothing is renamed in the timeline (the user's choice, 29 September 2026: a double-click there is two clicks), except a scene's name, which a double-click on it edits in place (the user's request, 30 September 2026: `beginRenaming`, an AppKit `NSTextField` over the label, Return or a click away keeping it, Escape the old name). Layers fold (30 September 2026): `EditorModel.foldedLayers` (per window, unsaved) makes `TimelineRow.rows(of:only:folded:)` give a folded layer one row where its first would be, with `folded` (every action of the layer, drawn on it together, each with an edge); `foldable` marks the first row of a layer with more than one action, which draws the chevron in the label's first `labelInset` points. Everything that looked at `row.action` goes through `row.actions` and `bar(at:in:)` (the bar on top under a point), so bars on a folded row hover, click, move, trim, marquee and snap as usual, but never reorder (`reordered` refuses); the folded row's name selects all its actions. `EditorModel.reveal(_:)` (from adding actions) opens the layers it adds to. Fold All Layers and Unfold All Layers are on the right-click menu. Scenes (30 September 2026, named by the user; "scene" no longer means the whole animation anywhere in the interface): `Project.scenes` (`TimelineScene`: id, name, `actionIDs`; left out of files when empty, `Project` decoding them if present) gathers actions from any layers, each in at most one; `EditorCommand.setScenes` replaces them, and `apply` drops ids of actions that went and scenes left empty. `Scenes` (MotionEditing, tested) groups (`Scene N`, the first free), ungroups, gives a `span` and `stretched` (each start and end keeping its share of the span, so moving is a stretch of the same length). `TimelineRow.rows(of:only:folded:foldedScenes:)` puts a scene's `header` row where its first action would be, its members after it (`inScene`, indented by `sceneIndent`, a line down the left), or only the header while in `EditorModel.foldedScenes` (per window); layers fold among their own rows in or out of a scene. The header draws its chevron, name, length and span bar (`drawScene`); `bar(at:)` ignores it, and its span is `spanRect`: a press picks all its actions and starts `Drag.shift` (all move) or, at an end, `Drag.stretch`. Colours: `Theme.timelineScene` (#2E5575, the user's choice: the span bar and the line down a scene's rows; scene rows have no background, as the user asked); folded layers have no colour of their own (the user took the slate tint away the same day), only the edge round each bar. A scene copied whole makes a scene of its own (the user's choice, 1 October 2026): `Scenes.whole` finds the scenes all of whose actions were copied, and `Scenes.copying` makes each one's copies a new scene named after it ("Scene 1 copy", then "Scene 1 copy 2", `copyBase`), one per group (the layer a paste went onto; nil for duplicates), sending the rest to `joining`; `ActionClipboard.scenes` and the layer clipboard's `scenes` carry the whole scenes, so this works after a cut and across projects, and a paste of a whole scene onto layers it did not come from keeps its times (`pasteActions`, `keepsTimes`) rather than going to the playhead. Copies of part of a scene join it (the user's choice, 30 September 2026): `Scenes.joining` takes (original, copy) pairs, from `ActionEditing.duplicates` by position, `ActionEditing.pastedPairs` and `Project.actionCopyPairs` (layer duplicates and pastes, which also keep `motionBlur` now), and the commands add a `.setScenes` through `sceneChange`. Group into Scene is a `PanelButton` in `ActionInspector` with two or more actions picked, and on the right-click menu with Rename Scene… (`SceneNameCard`) and Ungroup Scene. Command-click adds or takes away a bar; Shift-click adds every row from the last one clicked (`rangeAnchor`, set by clicks without Shift) to the one clicked. Trimming an end of one of several selected bars trims every selected bar with a length by the same time (`ActionEditing.trimmed(_:startEdge:by:...)`, each kept to its own limits), as one undo step (the user's choice, 28 September 2026). Cut, Copy, Paste (onto the right-clicked rows' layers), Duplicate, Stagger, Split into Actions, Show Only Selected Layers, Show Times as Frames and Show Sound at the Bottom are on the right-click menu (the last, `EditorModel.soundAtBottom`, remembered in defaults, the user's request on 1 October 2026: `TimelineRow.rows(soundLast:)` moves the rows of audio layers in no scene after all others, in their own order, without touching `rowOrder`; `reordered` refuses a drag across that line, since rows map to the global order), and a layer's right-click menu offers Paste Action in Animate when actions are copied. Copied actions (`ActionClipboard`) carry each one's `ActionOrigin`, where its layer stood as it began (`inheritedState`); `ActionEditing.pasted` puts them at the playhead on each target layer that takes them (presets by `Presets.available`, letter presets only on text), and on a layer other than their own `carried` makes the same motion from where the target stands (To values and starting values shifted by the difference in place, turn, skew and tilt, scaled by the ratio of scale and size; By values stay), the user's choice on 30 September 2026; `placingWithTheirLayers` puts each just under its layer's last row. Pasted presets never reached another layer until then, as `ActionFactory.kinds` has no preset; a duplicate (the menu or ⌘D) goes on the row just below its original (`ActionEditing.placingBelow`, renumbering the rows under it), later by the selection's length. Clicks on the ruler or empty timeline call `EditorController.glide(to:)` (a short eased slide on a timer, cancelled by any `seek`, scrub or play); drags call `seek`. `EditorModel.revealActions` (ids plus a request count, so the view never writes back) asks the tracks to scroll new rows into view; `addAction` sets it. Timing, trimming, reordering, stagger, duplicate and paste maths live in `MotionEditing/ActionEditing.swift` with tests. `editor.selectedActions` holds picked actions; `EditorController.selectedActions` only returns those whose layer is still selected. `timelineHasFocus` (Animate and the timeline clicked last) decides whether Delete, Copy, Paste and Duplicate act on actions or layers.
