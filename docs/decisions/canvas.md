# The canvas

Decisions worth knowing about the canvas, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 28 October 2026 so they load only when needed. Keep each entry up to date when its code changes.

- **The pen and point editing** (`Canvas/CanvasPathEditing.swift`, maths in `MotionEditing/PathEditing.swift`, tested): the canvas keeps a `PathSession` (the layer, its target, `.layer` in Design or `.action(id)` for a Path action in Animate, the picked `PointRef`s, and while drawing the open end `drawingFrom`). `validatePathSession` (from `show`) drops it when the selection, mode or tool moves on, and makes one whenever a single Path action is the `canvasAction`; it never writes to the model (finishing a drawing is sent on a turn later). Presses are `CanvasDrag.PathEdit`s that work from the path, layer and actions as they were at the press, with a 3-point dead zone so clicks never sprout handles. Every change to a layer's points goes through `PathEditing.reshaped(_:to:actions:)`: the box refits to the curves, the anchor keeps its fraction, the position moves so nothing shifts, `layerUpdate` carries Move actions, and the layer's Path actions are carried into the new space. Each pen click is its own undo step (begin at the press, end at release); the session re-reads the project, so undo mid-drawing just carries on from the new end. A new path is styled as a line (`LayerFactory.lineStroke`) and takes the default fill when closed, unless restyled (`styledForClosing`). The overlay draws points, handles and the pen's following segment (`CanvasOverlay.updatePath`); in Design the edited layer's box and handles hide. A Path action (`PathAction`, `path` channel) blends point by point and holds when point counts differ; its end is edited on the canvas at the layer's end-of-action place (`pathTransform`), points may move but not be added or deleted (Delete beeps). The test kit's `path xy` step logs points and their window coordinates.
- **Canvas**: `CanvasNSView` owns a `CoreAnimationRenderer` whose root sits in a `world` layer scaled and moved for zoom and pan; the selection is drawn by `CanvasOverlay` in view points. It renders from `show(...)`, which `updateNSView` calls, and never writes to the model there. A press anywhere along a selected layer's side (within `CanvasHandles.hitRadius`) resizes that side, not only its middle handle, as in Figma (5 October 2026: `CanvasHandles.handle(at:box:isLine:)` falls back to the nearest side, only while the layer is over `sideDepth` thick across it, so thin layers still move; lines keep their ends; cropping's box handles too). A plain drag elsewhere on a layer always moves it; moving the anchor needs Command, because the anchor sits where moves start. Option-drag in Design duplicates first (`EditorController.duplicateForDrag`, inside the drag's continuous change, so one undo) and drags the copies; a press that never moves cancels. Resizing a group changes its scale, not its size; resizing a frame changes its size. Shift on a layer already selected takes it out of the selection only when let go as a click (within `CanvasHandles.clickReach`, `CanvasDrag.Move.deselectsOnClick`), so a Shift-drag keeps it and moves the selection along one axis, as in Figma (4 October 2026; it was taken out at the press, leaving nothing to move). A click picks the outermost group (`inContext` in `layer(at:deep:)`) unless the selection is already inside one: then it picks the selected layer, a sibling of it, or what holds it, as Figma does; a double-click goes one level in. A press is a drag only once the pointer has gone `CanvasHandles.clickReach` (3 points) from where it went down (24 November 2026, `pastDeadZone` in `mouseDragged`): until then a move, resize, turn, anchor or action handle and a drawn shape do nothing, so a click that wobbles neither nudges the layer nor draws a one-pixel shape, and an Option-click that never left the dead zone makes no copy. Copy takes layers as they would stand at the top level (`Arrangement.move` to nil on a copy of the project, their actions carried too), since that is where a paste puts them, and the clipboard carries the media the layers use with each file's place, so a paste into another project stages the files it lacks (`pasteLayers`) and names any that have gone. The Text tool on an unlocked text types into it at the click, and only makes a text elsewhere (25 November 2026). `zoomToSelection` (Shift-2, handled in `keyDown` so it is not a menu key; View names it) fits the selected layers' corners into the canvas. A layer's right-click menu has Cut, Copy, Paste, Bring to Front and Send to Back, and Solo, Lock and Hide there and on a layers panel row take the whole selection when the layer is in it (`switched(by:)`); empty canvas has Paste, Select All and Zoom to Fit.
- **The tool bar** (`Editor/BottomBar.swift`): the tools straight on the floating bar, with no tray behind them (taken away on 30 September 2026, as the user asked: Flip needed it to part two rows of controls). One hover highlight (`GlideHover`, in `Components/Controls.swift`, shared with `SegmentRow`, every row of tabs, which glides its hover and its chosen grey the same way since 2 October 2026), drawn once behind the row at the hovered spot's box (`frames`, measured in the row's own coordinate space; `place` keeps its spot while it fades), slides from tool to tool, and to the shapes chevron, on `Theme.hoverGlide`, and appears in place when the pointer comes onto the bar; the tool in hand's accent is drawn the same way, over it, sliding on `.animation(Theme.hoverGlide, value: editor.tool)` however the tool is picked (`BarButton(onFill: .clear)`, so the button draws only its icon) (a highlight per tool handed on by a `matchedGeometryEffect` stuck going right to left, the fading one coming later in the row and pulling the new one back); leaving a spot waits `Theme.hoverLinger` before fading it, since the next spot's hover may arrive just after. Posted pointer moves do not reach `onHover`, so the kit cannot show the slide.
- **Canvas outlines**: a selected shape also shows its own outline in the accent (`CanvasOverlay.updateShapes`, from `selectedShapeOutlines`), so it reads when blurred or faded; not while its points are edited or it is tilted.
- **The anchor** is set in the Position section with `AnchorPicker` (nine points: corners, edge middles, centre, beside rotation), which moves the anchor and keeps the layer in place (`moveAnchor`); an anchor Command-dragged elsewhere on the canvas leaves no point chosen. There are no AX/AY fields.
- **Snapping and alignment**: the maths is `MotionEditing/Snapping.swift` (tested): `Snapping.move` (edges, centres, even spacing among boxes overlapping across the axis) and `Snapping.point` (a drawn corner or a resize handle), returning an offset plus `Guide`s and `Gap`s in composition space; `Alignment.align` and `.distribute` give per-layer moves. Everything works on upright boxes from `ResolvedScene.bounds(of:)` (a group's box is what is drawn inside it). The canvas gathers `snapTargets` when a drag begins, snaps in `mouseDragged` (not with ⌘, not when `EditorModel.snaps` is off, resize only when the layer sits upright and Shift is not held), and draws the result with `CanvasOverlay.updateSnap` in `Theme.guide`. Align and distribute are `EditorController.align`/`distribute`, Design only; their keys are `AlignKeys` on the canvas (by key code), named in the Arrange menu's titles, never menu keys, so Option-letter still types accents.
- **Several layers resized and turned together** (26 November 2026; `Canvas/CanvasGroupEditing.swift`): in Design, with several layers selected (`manyLayers`: the outermost, unlocked, none tilted or a template's slot), the box round them all is drawn with handles, and `beginManyEdit` comes before the layers under the press. A resize (`.resizeMany`) stands a plain rectangle in for the box and hands it to `CanvasGeometry.resized`, so the handles, Shift and Option behave exactly as one layer's; each layer's middle keeps its share of the box and its size takes the box's change, width and height each their own way when it lies square to the box (swapped when it lies a quarter turn round), one size for both when it is turned between; a group takes scale, a text only width. A turn (`.rotateMany`) is about the middle of the box, each layer's rotation going by as much (the other way inside a mirrored parent). Layers in different groups and frames are placed through their parents' world transforms. No snapping while resizing several. Animate still shows no box round several.
- **A box inside a frame** (26 November 2026, the user's request): a press that lands on a frame's own ground (the frame is what a plain click picks, it has layers, it is not a template or already selected, and `layer(at:deep:)` finds nothing deeper there) begins a marquee carrying `clickSelects`, not a move; `selectInMarquee` then picks among that frame's children, and a release without a drag selects the frame, as a click always did. A frame is hit anywhere in its box even with no fill (`hits` counts a closed shape with neither fill nor stroke), so before this every press in a scene held in one frame dragged the frame. A selected frame still drags, and Shift, Command and Option presses are as they were.
- **The hierarchy keys and flip** (26 November 2026): `CanvasNSView.hierarchyKeyDown`, after typing's keys and before a shape's (Return on a frame goes in, where it used to show the frame's points): Return is `selectChildren`, Shift-Return `selectParents`, Tab and Shift-Tab `selectSibling` (down or up the layers panel, round at the ends, past locked layers), all in `Editor/SelectionMoves.swift`. Return still types into a text and shows a shape's points, as neither holds layers. Shift-H and Shift-V are `flipSelection` (Design; also on the layer menu): that scale's sign and the rotation's change, and the position is put back so the middle of the box stays; each layer where it stands, not the selection as a whole.
- **Duplicate and paste** (26 November 2026): `duplicateSelectedLayers` remembers its copies and where their originals stood (`lastDuplicate`); duplicating exactly those copies after they have moved uses that move as the offset in place of 10, 10. `pasteLayers` puts what is pasted into the selection's group or frame, in front of the frontmost selected, when the whole selection shares one: inserted at the top level (where copied layers are made to stand) and then moved in with `Arrangement.move`, in one batch, so it stays where it was drawn and its actions follow.
- **Into and out of frames by dragging** (26 November 2026): letting go of a move over a frame the layers are not in (`dropFrame`: the front frame under the pointer, not locked, not a template or a slot, not being dragged nor inside what is) moves them into it with `Arrangement.move`, inside the move's own continuous change, so the drop and the move undo together and the layers stay where they were let go; over no frame they go to the top level. Only from the top level or out of a frame: a layer in a group that is not a frame stays the group's. Design only. While dragging, the frame it would go into is outlined as a hovered layer is (`dropTarget`).
- **Distances** (26 November 2026): with Option down (`measuring`, kept by `flagsChanged` and each `mouseMoved`), a selection and another layer under the pointer, `measureMarks` adds gap marks to the snapping overlay's (`updateSnap`): across the gap each way the two are apart, or from edge to matching edge where they overlap, in the composition's pixels. Nothing shows during a drag, where Option means a copy or a resize from the middle.
- **The Text tool's box** (26 November 2026): a press with the Text tool on anything but a text begins `.textBox`, drawn as the marquee is; letting go makes the text (`addText(width:)`), fixed to the dragged width when the drag went more than a few points across, growing as typed otherwise.
- **Quick keys** (26 November 2026, as Figma's): `CanvasNSView.quickKeyDown`, tried before the tools' keys. A number key is opacity in tens (0 is 100%), a second within 0.6 s making two digits (`opacityKey`); `]` and `[` are to the front and to the back; I is `pickFillFromScreen` (`NSColorSampler`, the top fill or a text's colour, each keeping its opacity); Shift-X `swapFillAndStroke` (the top fill and the top stroke; one alone becomes the other; a picture stays a fill); Option-/ and Shift-/ `removePaints`; Shift-1 and Shift-0 fit and actual size. Keys Shift or Option change the character of (1, 0, /) are read by key code. What changes a layer's design is Design's. Lock or Unlock is a menu key, ⇧⌘L; Hide or Show has a menu item and no key yet, since Figma's ⇧⌘H is Home here.
