# The canvas

Decisions worth knowing about the canvas, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 28 October 2026 so they load only when needed. Keep each entry up to date when its code changes.

- **The pen and point editing** (`Canvas/CanvasPathEditing.swift`, maths in `MotionEditing/PathEditing.swift`, tested): the canvas keeps a `PathSession` (the layer, its target, `.layer` in Design or `.action(id)` for a Path action in Animate, the picked `PointRef`s, and while drawing the open end `drawingFrom`). `validatePathSession` (from `show`) drops it when the selection, mode or tool moves on, and makes one whenever a single Path action is the `canvasAction`; it never writes to the model (finishing a drawing is sent on a turn later). Presses are `CanvasDrag.PathEdit`s that work from the path, layer and actions as they were at the press, with a 3-point dead zone so clicks never sprout handles. Every change to a layer's points goes through `PathEditing.reshaped(_:to:actions:)`: the box refits to the curves, the anchor keeps its fraction, the position moves so nothing shifts, `layerUpdate` carries Move actions, and the layer's Path actions are carried into the new space. Each pen click is its own undo step (begin at the press, end at release); the session re-reads the project, so undo mid-drawing just carries on from the new end. A new path is styled as a line (`LayerFactory.lineStroke`) and takes the default fill when closed, unless restyled (`styledForClosing`). The overlay draws points, handles and the pen's following segment (`CanvasOverlay.updatePath`); in Design the edited layer's box and handles hide. A Path action (`PathAction`, `path` channel) blends point by point and holds when point counts differ; its end is edited on the canvas at the layer's end-of-action place (`pathTransform`), points may move but not be added or deleted (Delete beeps). The test kit's `path xy` step logs points and their window coordinates.
- **Canvas**: `CanvasNSView` owns a `CoreAnimationRenderer` whose root sits in a `world` layer scaled and moved for zoom and pan; the selection is drawn by `CanvasOverlay` in view points. It renders from `show(...)`, which `updateNSView` calls, and never writes to the model there. A press anywhere along a selected layer's side (within `CanvasHandles.hitRadius`) resizes that side, not only its middle handle, as in Figma (5 October 2026: `CanvasHandles.handle(at:box:isLine:)` falls back to the nearest side, only while the layer is over `sideDepth` thick across it, so thin layers still move; lines keep their ends; cropping's box handles too). A plain drag elsewhere on a layer always moves it; moving the anchor needs Command, because the anchor sits where moves start. Option-drag in Design duplicates first (`EditorController.duplicateForDrag`, inside the drag's continuous change, so one undo) and drags the copies; a press that never moves cancels. Resizing a group changes its scale, not its size; resizing a frame changes its size. Shift on a layer already selected takes it out of the selection only when let go as a click (within `CanvasHandles.clickReach`, `CanvasDrag.Move.deselectsOnClick`), so a Shift-drag keeps it and moves the selection along one axis, as in Figma (4 October 2026; it was taken out at the press, leaving nothing to move). A click picks the outermost group (`inContext` in `layer(at:deep:)`) unless the selection is already inside one: then it picks the selected layer, a sibling of it, or what holds it, as Figma does; a double-click goes one level in.
- **The tool bar** (`Editor/BottomBar.swift`): the tools straight on the floating bar, with no tray behind them (taken away on 30 September 2026, as the user asked: Flip needed it to part two rows of controls). One hover highlight (`GlideHover`, in `Components/Controls.swift`, shared with `SegmentRow`, every row of tabs, which glides its hover and its chosen grey the same way since 2 October 2026), drawn once behind the row at the hovered spot's box (`frames`, measured in the row's own coordinate space; `place` keeps its spot while it fades), slides from tool to tool, and to the shapes chevron, on `Theme.hoverGlide`, and appears in place when the pointer comes onto the bar; the tool in hand's accent is drawn the same way, over it, sliding on `.animation(Theme.hoverGlide, value: editor.tool)` however the tool is picked (`BarButton(onFill: .clear)`, so the button draws only its icon) (a highlight per tool handed on by a `matchedGeometryEffect` stuck going right to left, the fading one coming later in the row and pulling the new one back); leaving a spot waits `Theme.hoverLinger` before fading it, since the next spot's hover may arrive just after. Posted pointer moves do not reach `onHover`, so the kit cannot show the slide.
- **Canvas outlines**: a selected shape also shows its own outline in the accent (`CanvasOverlay.updateShapes`, from `selectedShapeOutlines`), so it reads when blurred or faded; not while its points are edited or it is tilted.
- **The anchor** is set in the Position section with `AnchorPicker` (nine points: corners, edge middles, centre, beside rotation), which moves the anchor and keeps the layer in place (`moveAnchor`); an anchor Command-dragged elsewhere on the canvas leaves no point chosen. There are no AX/AY fields.
- **Snapping and alignment**: the maths is `MotionEditing/Snapping.swift` (tested): `Snapping.move` (edges, centres, even spacing among boxes overlapping across the axis) and `Snapping.point` (a drawn corner or a resize handle), returning an offset plus `Guide`s and `Gap`s in composition space; `Alignment.align` and `.distribute` give per-layer moves. Everything works on upright boxes from `ResolvedScene.bounds(of:)` (a group's box is what is drawn inside it). The canvas gathers `snapTargets` when a drag begins, snaps in `mouseDragged` (not with ⌘, not when `EditorModel.snaps` is off, resize only when the layer sits upright and Shift is not held), and draws the result with `CanvasOverlay.updateSnap` in `Theme.guide`. Align and distribute are `EditorController.align`/`distribute`, Design only; their keys are `AlignKeys` on the canvas (by key code), named in the Arrange menu's titles, never menu keys, so Option-letter still types accents.
