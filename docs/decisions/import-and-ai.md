# Importing and AI apps

Decisions worth knowing about importing and AI apps, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 28 October 2026 so they load only when needed. Keep each entry up to date when its code changes.

- **AI apps through MCP** (19 October 2026, the user's request): Claude, Cursor and any app speaking the Model Context Protocol work in the open project. The app listens on a Unix socket, `AgentSocket.url` (`~/Library/Application Support/<bundle id>/agent.sock`, folder 0700, socket 0600, so only this user; the test copy's id gives it its own), while `AppSettings.allowsAgents` is on (the default; Settings' AI apps card, with a Copy Command button for `claude mcp add`; its rows show Hugeicons' MCP and Claude logos, `Icon.mcp` and `Icon.claude`). AI apps start the bridge, Twine's own program with `--mcp` (`AgentBridge`, checked first thing in `TwineMain.main`, before any AppKit setup): it pipes stdin lines to the socket and replies to stdout, opens Twine with `open -g` when nothing answers (30 s for it to start, 4 when it was running with AI apps off), reconnects after Twine quits (closing the window quits it), and answers any request Twine never replied to with an error. `MCPServer` keeps no state between messages, so a reconnect needs no second handshake. `AgentService` (`Twine/Agent`) accepts on its own queue and answers on the main actor. `TwineAgent` (MotionAgent) has 20 tools: status, projects (list, open, new, from a template), `get_project` (an outline, `ProjectOutline`: layers front first with x, y as the box's top-left in the parent, and actions with their values; or file JSON by id), `describe` (worked out from `ActionFactory`, `Presets.library`, `Templates.library`, so never stale), add, update, delete and arrange layers, add and update actions and presets, templates, composition, view (mode, playhead, selection), undo, `render_frame` (a PNG through an `OffscreenRenderer`, with the editor's pictures and videos of its own), `import_media` and `export` (both awaited: `importFiles` and `importSVG` give their layers, `setTemplateMedia` its Task). Edits reach the file format through JSON merge patches (`ModelJSON.patched`: the id is kept, new fills, strokes and effects get ids and defaults, `"#RRGGBB"` is read where a colour goes, and the result goes through `FileSafety.numbersHeld` and `held` as any file does); moves carry Move actions (`Project.updating`, which `EditorController.layerUpdate` now calls). `AppAgentHost.perform` posts an `.applicationDefined` event after each change: a change from outside a click or key press otherwise leaves the undo group open, the document never counts as edited and nothing saves (the first live test lost every change). `MotionAgentTests` cover the tools on a `ProjectStore` host and the protocol; live, drive the test copy's bridge with lines of JSON (`TwineTest --mcp`), launched first with `TWINE_CREATIONS`, since a bridge that relaunches it passes no environment and it would use its default projects folder. The bridge shares the app's program, so `pgrep -x Twine` (or `TwineTest`) also finds bridges.
- **The Figma plugin** (`FigmaPlugin/`, its README says how it is added and built): `code.js` describes the selection as a `twine.figma` payload (JSON on the clipboard, copied by the panel through a hidden text box), each layer with its absolute transform so Figma's groups need no care, and flattens what Twine cannot hold while Figma can still draw it: `picture` (PNG at 2×, placed at its render bounds) for mixed-style, italic, decorated or list text, diamond gradients, picture filters or odd turns (angular gradients come in as Twine's since 4 October 2026, turned the other way round when Figma's transform mirrors them), luminance masks and unknown types; `svg` (Figma's SVG, read by `SVGImport` into a group) for vectors with painted regions or mixed winding, and smoothed rectangles. A rounded polygon travels as a polygon with its radius, and a vector's rounded points as `corners` (`[x, y, radius]` from its vector network's vertices, or the vector's own radius; `vectorPaths` are sharp), which `FigmaImport.rounding` puts on the points at those places. Frames are never flattened (their children would be); a paint they cannot keep and uneven borders are left out with a `warnings` line. A layer keeps one picture fill, the topmost shown (`keptPaints` in the plugin, so the others are not copied, and `FigmaImport.fills`): pictures stacked under it cannot be seen. A frame's picture background comes in as its own `Background` picture layer at the bottom of the frame, its size and corners, with any fills Figma drew over it (`FigmaImport.background`), so it shows in the layers panel; the fills under it stay the frame's. `FigmaImport` (MotionImport) builds the layers: transforms into each parent's space (Figma's turn is anticlockwise, so the matrix's own `atan2(b, a)` is Twine's; `place` splits any matrix into Twine's turn, skew across and scale, so skewed layers, frames and text come in as themselves since 28 September 2026, and the plugin no longer flattens them), masks as they are (Figma's and Twine's are the same), gradients and picture crops through the inverse of Figma's transforms, font style names to weights, fonts this Mac lacks noted. `EditorController.pasteFromFigma` (from Paste when the clipboard string is a payload): it is centred at its exact size, never shrunk, and the composition is never resized to fit it, even when empty (both the user's choice; the empty-project resize went on 28 September 2026); pictures are staged and reused by hash; one undo step; missing fonts and warnings go to `EditorModel.showNotice` (`NoticeCard`, six seconds). The notice is a `Notice` (text and a state: a note, work under way with an optional fraction, or done). Imports follow their work through it (the user's request, 30 September 2026): `beginWork(_:after:)` (0.4 s, so quick imports show nothing; at once for a WebM) shows a `Loader` ring, `updateWork` sets the text and how far (a WebM's conversion from ffmpeg's `-progress` lines, `out_time_us` against the probed duration), `finishWork(done:)` shows a check and the done text for three seconds, or with nil clears it; `showNotice` drops any work so an error is never written over, and the close button (`dismissNotice`) hides the work's notice for good. `FigmaPlugin/test/run.js` runs `code.js` on a stand-in for Figma and writes `test/sample-payload.json`, which `FigmaPluginSampleTests` reads. The panel is drawn as the app's panels are (28 September 2026; the design is on Page 3, "Twine plugin states", of Figma file `RRnath7INm7WH7CGLOfLdH`): a floating panel on the canvas colour with no header (Figma's window title says Twine), a preview well showing the selection as Figma draws it (`sendPreview`: each selected layer exported as PNG and laid out where it sits, newest selection wins), its name with its size and layer count, and the blue Copy button; copying shows a Newsreader percentage and a progress bar, and a copy a check in the well's top-right corner. `ui.src.html` is built into `ui.html` by `build.py`. Figma's manifest has no icon field, so a development plugin shows Figma's own icon; `FigmaPlugin/icon.png` (the app icon at 128) is for the publish dialog, the only place an icon is set. Figma reads a development plugin's files from its folder on every run; moving the folder means importing the manifest again (the rename to Twine did). Not yet run inside Figma.
- **SVG import** (`MotionImport`, tested in `MotionImportTests`, including a pixel comparison of Twine's drawing against the system's): the file is read with `XMLDocument` and walked with each element's full transform, so every layer is made in the SVG's own space, scaled to its final size (fitted to the composition), and the file comes in as a frame (no fill, clipping, as a viewport does) named after it. Rectangles and ellipses whose transform only moves, turns and scales keep their kind; everything else becomes a path drawn through its transform (`SVGPathData`: every command, quadratics raised, arcs cut into quarter-turn cubics). Styles follow SVG's order (attributes, `.class` rules from `<style>`, `style`), inheriting what inherits. Gradients (either units, `href` chains, `gradientTransform`) are mapped into the layer's box; `fill-rule` becomes `ShapeContent.fillRule` (even-odd is drawn with the rule and normalised into a plain area, `ResolvedShape.area`, wherever paths are cut or joined); groups of one with nothing of their own flatten. `clip-path` and alpha (or white luminance) masks become a mask layer in a Mask group with the masked one; both at once, or a mask Twine cannot draw, and the element becomes a picture. Figma's filters are read by their result names (`SVGFilters`) into drop and inner shadows and layer blur; its background blur comes from the `foreignObject` before the shape. Figma's picture fills (a `pattern` of one `use` of an embedded `image`) become picture fills with the crop; plain `<image>`s become picture rectangles. Text is drawn by `SVGPictures` (AppKit's own SVG drawing, which draws no filters, misplaces positioned `tspan`s, so each becomes a `<text>`, and reads font weights only as numbers) from an SVG of just that element inside its ancestors' styles, with every definition, trimmed to its ink and placed there as a picture. `Result.reusing` swaps in pictures the project has. `EditorController.importSVG` stages the pictures (`ProjectDocument.stage(_ data:as:)`) and inserts the frame as one undo step; `importMedia` sends `.svg` files there. Hostile files are refused or read safely (`HostileImportTests`, 28 September 2026): entities that are files, web addresses, parameter entities or made of others are refused before parsing (`declaresRiskyEntities`; Illustrator's plain ones pass) and external entities are never loaded; nesting is counted from the bytes before `XMLDocument` sees them (`nesting(of:limit:)`), since the system's reader itself overflows its stack about a hundred thousand deep; past `maxDepth` (256, a `use` counting as a level) or `maxElements` (100,000 read, repeats included) the file is `ReadError.tooComplex`; a `use` never expands itself or what holds it (`expanding`); every number read goes through `SVGNumber.sane` (no `nan`, `inf` or anything past `limit`, a billion), and `Finite.layers` leaves out any layer whose place, size or points still ran past it, for Figma pastes too, whose polygons and stars keep at most `FigmaImport.maxPoints` (60, as the inspector).
