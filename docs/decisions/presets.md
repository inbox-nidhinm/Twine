# Presets

Decisions worth knowing about presets, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 28 October 2026 so they load only when needed. Keep each entry up to date when its code changes.

- **Presets** are one bar each, as in Jitter (the user's choice on 28 September 2026, replacing presets that added their separate actions): `ActionSpec.preset(PresetAction)` stores the `PresetKind` and its settings (direction, distance, scale, angle, blur, fades; nil is the preset's default). The recipe is `PresetRecipe` (MotionEvaluation): `defaults`, `length`, `easing`, `usesEasing` (Shake and Wiggle keep their jolts' own, so the inspector offers no easing), and `parts`, each a `PresetEffect` timed as fractions of the bar, so stretching the bar stretches every part; a part's `easing` nil follows the bar's. `ActionTracks` puts a preset on every channel its parts reach and turns each part into a `Step` whose values come from a `PartRun` (rule 8 in `walk`): from what the step inherits, what the preset found on that channel at its first part (`own`, shared through `PartBox.group`) and the design value, so an entrance lands where the layer was and a faded-out layer fades back to its design; a part that sets its start holds it from the scene's start as an explicit initial value does. Only layers with a preset take this way (`presetLayers`), and the part data is boxed, because a larger `Step` made every frame's evaluation a third slower; evaluate is 1.22 ms against 1.19 before. `Action.drives(_:)` says whether an action reaches a channel (sound fades use it). `Presets` (MotionEditing, tested) is the list (`library`, in headed `PresetGroup`s since the user asked for Jitter's grouping later on 28 September 2026, replacing the earlier no-groups list: Fade (Fade, Slide, Drop), Scale (Grow, Shrink, Spin, Twist, Move & Scale), Mask (Mask Reveal, Center, Resize, & Slide, Shrink), Blur (Blur, & Slide, & Scale), Emphasis, Text, Sound, the library running group by group; a preset that brings a layer on and its twin that takes it off are one entry, listed by the In kind; `PresetKind.reversed` pairs them, `entering` says which way, `Presets.name(of:)` is Fade In or Fade Out; the preset's settings have an In / Out switch that turns the bar, carrying its name, easing and length along when still the preset's own; Rotate Out and Drop Out were added so every pair is whole), `controls(for:)` (what the inspector's `PresetFields` shows), `available`, `action` (one bar at the playhead, at the preset's length and easing, named after it) and `split` (Split into Actions on the timeline's right-click menu, `EditorController.splitSelectedPresets`: the same motion as ordinary actions fixed from `ActionTracks.inheritedState`, which for a preset is the layer at its start without it). Presets added before 28 September 2026 went in as their actions and stay so. Animate's inspector with layers but no actions selected shows two tiles (`ActionTiles` in `Editor/PresetPicker.swift`): New Action raises the action menu, Select Preset puts the `PresetPicker` in the inspector's place (a search box first, taking the keyboard as it opens, which narrows the tiles by name, Out name or group, `matching`, Return adding the first shown, 30 September 2026; one grid, a pair's tile playing In then Out; picking adds the preset, whose settings then show; the close button or a new selection goes back). Each tile plays its preset on a rounded square (`PresetThumbnail`), from `PresetPreview`: a one-layer scene run through `ActionTracks` itself, so a tile always shows what the preset really does; sound presets show level bars. `test.sh` fails when the tests fail or do not build (it used to hide both behind its filter). `EditorController.addPreset` and `addAction` share `insertAndShow` (bottom rows, select, reveal, glide to the end).
- **The 3D presets** (17 October 2026, templates milestone 4, `PresetGroup.space`, titled 3D, after Flip): Flip (`.turn` parts, both tilt angles; the side named goes first, `flipTurn`), Swing (`turnPage` hinged on an edge, Elastic in), Fold (`.bend` parts from `foldBend`, whole layer only, `movesWholeLayer`), Tumble (turn, rotate and `.depth` together), Fly (`.depth` from 1200 away, out 600 towards you), Iso Lift (tilt x 60, rotate and move, as lying on a floor), and the emphases Spin 3D and Float. `.turn`, `.depth` and `.bend` are `PresetEffect`s with `tiltPart`, `depthPart` and `bendPart` in `ActionTracks`, so depth and bend channels walk preset parts too, and `Presets.split` makes Tilt, Depth and Bend actions of them. Settings: direction (named Hinge, Turn or Curve by `directionLabel`), angle, distance, fades. The picker's tiles draw z and bend point by point (`Camera3D.ownPoints`, the path flattened and cut into short pieces, `carried`).
- **Mask presets and reveals** (28 September 2026): a Mask preset clips the layer to its own box through the `reveal` channel (`PresetEffect.reveal`, `LayerState.reveal`, `ResolvedLayer.reveal`): a `RevealBox` (MotionEvaluation, `Reveal.swift`) is the open part of the box and the content's shift and zoom behind it, all as fractions of the box, boxed in `Reveal` (`.none` when the whole box shows with nothing moved, so a finished preset leaves no clip). Only presets drive it, so a Mask preset cannot be split (`Presets.splits`, which `canSplitPresets` asks). The renderer makes two holders the first time a node is clipped (`Node.updateReveal`, `RevealLayers.apply`: a clip with its bounds in the layer's own coordinates, masking to bounds, and inside it the content, moved and zoomed about the centre) and from then on puts the node's sublayers in them (`Node.host`); it leaves `container.mask` to layer masks. Twist turns in depth with a tilt part (`PresetEffect.tilt`).
- **The Flip group** (1 October 2026, the user's request; Page Turn first by mistake for the curl, kept by the user's choice): **Page Turn** (`pageTurnIn`/`Out`, `PresetEffect.turnPage`, the `tilt` channel) swings the layer on one edge like the onboarding's leaf: `Tilt.hinge` (a share of the box, nil for every other tilt, left out of files) makes `Projective.layer` turn about that point along the layer's own rotation, seen from `Tilt.hingeDistance` (1 / 0.35, the onboarding's perspective) times the longer side; Out lifts the free edge towards the viewer to edge-on, In comes down from the far side (`ActionTracks.pageHinge`), and its split keeps the hinge in To values. **Page Curl** (`pageCurlIn`/`Out`, `PresetEffect.curl`, the `curl` channel, `PresetAction.corner`, the inspector's Corner row with the `corner...` icons) is Flip's curl: Core Image's `CIPageCurlWithShadowTransition` (Flip's `PageCurl.swift`) set with a `CICrop` in the container's `filters` (`PageCurlFilter`, MotionRendering), over a clear target, so whatever is under the layer shows; the back is the top fill's colour lifted 28% towards white (`lifted`), drawn in the layer's outline. `Curl` boxes `PageCurl` (progress and corner) as `Reveal` does; `resolve` turns one curled right off into opacity 0. The filter sees layer coordinates running down on the canvas and off screen alike (`angle(_:runsDown:)`; a picture for the picker's tile runs up). Easing is Flip's SwiftUI ease in and out, a custom (0.42, 0, 0.58, 1); the app's Smooth front-loads it. A curl cannot be split or put on letters (`PresetKind.movesWholeLayer`). `aPageCurlLiftsItsCornerAndStaysInItsBox` checks pixels.
- **Text presets, letter by letter** (28 September 2026): any preset but the sound ones can move a text's letters, words or lines one by one (`PresetAction.unit`, `order`, `delay`; nil unit moves the whole layer), set in the inspector's Text animation rows (Apply to, Order, Delay in ms) on text layers; the Text group's presets (Typewriter, Letters Rise, Letters Pop, Words Blur, Lines Reveal) go on that way, for text only, their bars named after them and longer (`Preset.text`, `Preset.length`). Such a bar never touches the layer's channels: `ActionTracks` keeps it in `textPresets` with its recipe played on a stand-in letter (`TextPreset`, `TextAnimation.stand`, at rest `TextAnimation.rest`), and `textAnimation(of:at:)` gives the one in force (the latest started, or before any the first; each ends with every letter home, so handing over shows nothing). `TextAnimation.states(count:at:)` times each letter: the bar is the whole animation, each starts `delay` after the one before in the order (`ranks`: forward, backward, from centre, from edges, random seeded by the bar's id) and runs the recipe in what is left, the delay shortened when a letter would get under a quarter of the bar. `TextSpans` (MotionEvaluation) splits a set text into letters (composed characters, no spaces), words or lines with their boxes from the same layout. `resolve` fills `ResolvedText.units` (`ResolvedTextUnit`: range, box, a `Projective` about its centre, opacity, blur, reveal), empty once every letter is at rest; the renderer then draws each unit from a `TextImage` of just its own glyphs (`TextImage(key, unit:)`, its box with a margin all round, cached per text; it once drew every line touching the box and so carried slivers of the lines above and below, `aMovingLineCarriesNothingOfItsNeighbours`) in a `TextUnitLayer` instead of the whole text. Typewriter is its own pair (`typewriterIn`, `typewriterOut`, 29 September 2026; it was a Fade In on letters, which faded each letter over at least a quarter of the bar): an instant opacity part (`PresetRecipe.isInstant`), so `states(count:at:)` spreads the turns evenly from the bar's start to its end whatever the delay, and the inspector hides Delay and Easing for it; a Text-only pair is named by its Text preset (Typewriter, Typewriter Out, `Presets.name(of:)`); `Presets.withTypewriters` (from `ProjectDocument.read`) turns older Typewriter bars still at their settings into it. A preset put on letters travels the type size (`Presets.unitDistance`, also when Apply to changes and the distance was still its own). The picker's tiles play text presets on five small squares. With text selected the picker puts the Text group first (`PresetPicker.groups`). A preset's direction is chosen from arrows pointing to the side (`SegmentRow(icon:)`, the word as the tooltip; the designer supplied no down arrow, so `arrow-down.svg` is the up one turned over).
